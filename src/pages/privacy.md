# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

This policy covers **dartway.dev** and the small tools DartWay runs for its own publishing.
DartWay is operated by Evgenii Novikov, Batumi, Georgia.

## This website

- **No cookies, no accounts, no personal data.** The site does not set cookies and has no sign-in.
- **A page counter.** When a page opens, the site sends the page path, the referring page and the
  type of event (a pageview or a click on a call-to-action button) to our own counter. IP addresses
  are not stored, and nothing identifies you or links one visit to another. The numbers tell us which
  pages are read and where readers come from — nothing about who they are.
- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; the counter runs on Cloudflare. Both process requests
  under their own privacy policies.

## LinkedIn publishing

DartWay has a LinkedIn application used by one person — the operator — to publish posts from
his own Telegram channels and articles to his own LinkedIn profile. No one else signs in to it.

- **What it receives from LinkedIn:** the operator's name and LinkedIn member ID (via
  Sign In with LinkedIn) and permission to post on his behalf (Share on LinkedIn).
- **What it does with it:** publishes posts the operator has approved. It does not read your feed,
  profile, connections or messages, and has no access to them.
- **Where it is kept:** the access token and member ID are stored on the operator's own machine
  and are not shared, sold or sent anywhere except to LinkedIn itself.
- **How to revoke:** access can be withdrawn at any time in LinkedIn settings under
  _Data privacy → Permitted services_, after which the token stops working.
